Bots
Every bot starts in dry-run. Start / Pause / Stop act on the trading loop through the bot's API; a managed bot's process runs as the user unit freqtrade-bot@<name>. The kill switch pauses every bot and resume starts only the ones it paused. A bot can trade live only after it passes every check under Details → Live trading.

 News feeds 

            

              

                
FeedKindCategoryLast fetchItemsStoredLatest articleOnActions

                
              

            

            

              
              
              
              
              
            

            
The news job reads every enabled feed every 15 minutes (https only, up to 3 MB), keeps 30 days, drops headlines already seen from another feed, and tags each article with the assets it names: tickers in capitals (BTC, $AAPL), common coin names (Bitcoin, Ether, Solana…) and the names of registered stocks. A per-symbol feed is read once for each active TradFi instrument, with {ticker} in its URL. The tone dot is a keyword count (surge, rally… vs. plunge, hack…), not a model.

          

        


        

          

            
 Trading venues
Exchange and broker accounts: keys, connection tests

            

              

                
VenueKindExchangeMarketKeys forAPI keysLast testOnActions

                
              

            

            

              
              
              
              
              
              
              
            

            
Crypto exchanges are traded through Freqtrade (ccxt); the list below the form is Freqtrade's own. API keys are written to ~/.openclaw/credentials/venues/<id>.env (0600) and never shown again, only their last four characters. Test reads the markets and a ticker, and with keys the balance, in a sandboxed unit; it never orders or withdraws. Use exchange keys without withdrawal rights, limited to this server's IP. A managed bot takes a venue's keys under Strategies → Details → Live trading. Alpaca connects in paper mode only (its paper-trading API).

Convert to a Freqtrade strategy

      

        
        
        
      

      

      

      
Variables become columns, inputs become hyperopt parameters, and strategy.entry / strategy.close become entry and exit signals (Freqtrade also enters at the next candle's open). Stoploss and ROI are not converted: the class starts with a 10 % stoploss and no ROI exits; adjust them in the strategy editor. The strategy is saved to the library and checked.
